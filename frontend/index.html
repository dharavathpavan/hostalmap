How Our Experience Rating Works

Unlike generic review portals, Hostel Map calculates a weighted experience score prioritizing what genuinely matters to students living away from home:

🍲 Food Quality (25%): Taste, hygiene, daily variety, and timely meal serving.
✨ Cleanliness (20%): Room sweeping, washroom sanitation, and trash disposal.
🛡️ Safety & Security (20%): Biometric logs, CCTV, warden support, and campus safety.
🤝 Management (15%): Rapid maintenance response, fair deposit refunds.
📶 WiFi & Facilities (10%): High speed internet for study/work, laundry, power backup.
💰 Value for Money (10%): Transparency of electricity and maintenance charges.

Recency Weighting: Reviews posted in the last 90 days are weighted 1.5× higher to reflect current food chefs and warden management.

Anonymous Privacy: Your identity is strictly anonymous to prevent hostel management harassment.
